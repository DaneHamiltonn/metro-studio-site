## The brief

Diamanté Cherubs makes handmade sleepwear and occasion wear for babies and children, each piece made to order. Running a small business means the owner needs to manage the shop from anywhere, not just from a desk.

## What we did

We built a full e-commerce site on Squarespace, with a soft, nostalgic brand to match. Lush botanical illustration and blush pink frame lifestyle photography, and the copy feels like a family tradition. The shop is organised by collection, girls, boys and clearance, with size guides and made-to-order lead times on every product.

We set the store up so it can be run day to day from the Squarespace mobile app. Orders, stock and product updates can all be handled from a phone.

## Seasonal marketing

The year revolves around the seasons: Christmas pyjamas and Christmas Eve boxes, Easter jam pants sets and summer gingham. We plan and run the seasonal marketing and keep the inventory in step with each new collection.
