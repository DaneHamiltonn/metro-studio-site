## The brief

The Irish Guards Association's North of England Branch brings together serving and former Guardsmen, "irrespective of rank, circumstances or nationality". The branch had a strong identity and a loyal membership, but its presence needed bringing up to date. It had to feel modern without losing the heritage that makes the regiment what it is.

## What we did

We started with the brand. We refined how the regimental badge, colours and typography work together, so they hold up on a phone screen as well as on a printed dinner menu.

Then we designed and built a new website from scratch and wrote the content with the committee: membership, events, news, the journal archive, welfare support and the committee itself. The result is a warm, confident home for the "regimental family" that members actually use.

## Taking the annual dinner online

The annual dinner is the highlight of the branch's year. Ordering used to be handled by hand. We digitised the whole process: members choose their options and pay online, and all their details are passed straight to the branch, ready for the evening.

## Ongoing

Since 2018 we've managed the website and brand day to day. We handle content updates, event listings, marketing and new features as the branch's needs change.
