## The brief

Primas Consultancy set out to support Further Education institutions, with the learner at the heart of everything it did. It needed a brand that felt energetic and optimistic, a long way from the usual grey of education consultancy.

## What we did

We created the whole identity: a simple "P" monogram, a bright teal and bold diagonal brushstrokes that bring movement and energy to black-and-white photography. The Webflow website introduces the firm, sets out its services as four clear pillars (Teaching, Learning, Assessment and Development) and keeps visitors up to date with the latest news from the sector.

## The outcome

The business closed before it launched, so the site was never fully released. It remains a complete brand and web concept, and one we're still proud of.
