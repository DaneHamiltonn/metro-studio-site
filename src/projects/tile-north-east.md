## The brief

Tiles aren't like most products. People choose them by room, compare them by finish and size, and buy them by the square metre, the pack or the pallet. Tile North East needed a refreshed brand and an online shop that understood all of that.

## What we did

We refreshed the brand with a crisp, architectural look that lets the products be the hero. Then we built a custom e-commerce site on Webflow, structured specifically for tiles: ranges by bathroom, kitchen and floor, with prices shown per tile, per m² and per pack. Accessories and pallet deals sit alongside the main ranges.

## Ongoing

We manage the inventory and keep the range current, adding new lines, sale items and featured products as stock changes.
