## The brief

Platform Logic implements, optimises and manages Microsoft Dynamics 365 and the Power Platform. Its work runs from diagnosis and rapid builds to recruitment, managed services and training. It's a fast-moving business, so it needed a brand and website that could move just as fast.

## What we did

We designed a bold, dark identity with an electric gradient that nods to data, connection and the Microsoft ecosystem without copying it. On the website, a complicated offer becomes easy to navigate: products such as Dynamics 365, Power BI and Copilot sit alongside services from Platform Diagnose to Platform Academy, each with its own page.

## An agile partnership

Platform Logic doesn't stand still, so neither do we. We manage the site's content and turn new pages, training courses and campaigns around quickly. A new two-day Copilot course, for example, can go from idea to live promotion without waiting weeks.
