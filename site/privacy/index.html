Privacy

Your information stays yours.

We build apps that need as little of your information as possible. This page explains, in plain English, what each one uses and why.

Last updated 16 November 2026

Enroute

  • No account is needed to track trains, and Enroute never asks for your email address.
  • Your journeys are stored on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, synced through your own private iCloud account. Metro Studio does not hold a database of anyone's travel.
  • Location is used once, only when you ask for departures near you, to find your nearest station. It isn't stored or shared.
  • Sign in with Apple is optional and only used for Friends. Sign-in details are kept in your device's Keychain, and you can revoke access at any time.
  • Friends see the train you choose to share (its route, estimated position and status), never your own location. Sharing is read-only and you can remove anyone's access at any moment.

MyWashingLine

  • No account and no sign-up.
  • Location is requested only while you're using the app, to show the forecast where you are. You can search for a place instead.
  • Weather comes from MET Norway. To fetch a forecast, the app sends them the place's coordinates, rounded to four decimal places. No personal details are sent.
  • Saved places are stored on your device only. There is no cloud sync.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies, advertising or tracking. If you email us, we'll use your message only to reply to you.
