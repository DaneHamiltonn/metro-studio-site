Our story

Started at thirteen.
Still sweating the details.

Metro Studio began twelve years ago as a teenager's design project in Newcastle. It's grown up a lot since, but the obsession with getting things just right hasn't changed.

  1. The beginning

    Metro Architectural Studios

    It started with buildings: drawings, plans and a love of structure. You can still see it in our triangulated M.

  2. The change

    Metro Studio

    From buildings to brands. Identity, graphics and video for businesses, with analytics at the core.

  3. Today

    Our own apps

    Enroute and MyWashingLine: the craft we bring to client work, put into products of our own.
