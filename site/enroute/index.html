Journeys

A map of everywhere you're going.

Every upcoming journey drawn across Britain, with your train moving along its route. The one under way gets the spotlight; everything after is a simple countdown.

Your train

Everything about your train. Honestly.

Delays shown with the timetable struck through. Every stop on the route. And a platform that never pretends to be more certain than it is.

  • See where your inbound train is before it reaches you
  • Position between stations is shown as an estimate, never as GPS
  • Every screen tells you how fresh the information is
Lock Screen & Dynamic Island

Your journey, without opening the app.

A Live Activity follows your train: platform, expected times, delay and progress. Notifications arrive for the moments that matter, once each.

  • Platform confirmed
  • Your train is in
  • Arriving at your stop
Departures

The board for the station you're standing in.

One tap shows departures from your nearest station. Track any train in three taps. Your location is used once, to find the station, and never stored.

Friends

Know when to leave for the station.

Share your train with whoever's picking you up. They see the route, the train's estimated position and when it's due in. They never see you.

  • Share every journey, or choose each one
  • Hide any trip with one switch. Perfect for a surprise visit
  • Invite by iMessage; remove access at any moment
Passport

Every journey, kept for good.

When a trip ends, Enroute keeps a permanent record: timetable and actual times side by side, the route and the platforms. Your history never depends on a railway server.

  • Statistics that only count what was really recorded
  • Delay Repay guidance when you arrive 15+ minutes late
Yours, and private

We don't keep a database of anyone's travel.

No account needed

Track trains straight away. Enroute never asks for your email address.

Your own iCloud

Journeys sync through your private iCloud, not our servers.

Works offline

Your journeys are on your iPhone, with or without signal.
